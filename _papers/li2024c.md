---
title: "Toward An Analytic Theory of Intrinsic Robustness for Dexterous Grasping"

date: 2024-03-12

pdf: https://arxiv.org/pdf/2403.07249
location: IEEE/RSJ International Conference on Intelligent Robots and Systems (IROS)
---
We develop an analytic theory of grasp robustness to intrinsic uncertainty, such as object pose or geometry, by characterizing how friction cone uncertainty affects force closure.
